Bring! for Claude

Let Claude read and update your Bring! shopping lists, on your phone and on claude.ai.

This link is incomplete. Ask the person who sent it for the full sign-up link.

1. Sign in with your Bring! account

Your password is used once to sign in to Bring and is not stored. Accounts that use only Google or Apple sign-in need to set a password in the Bring app first.

Your personal connector link

Keep it private: anyone with this link can change your lists.

2. Add it to Claude

  1. On a computer or phone browser, open claude.ai → Settings → Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector, name it Bring, and paste your link as the URL. Leave the OAuth fields empty.
  3. Close and reopen the Claude app on your phone. In a chat, tap + and turn on Bring.
  4. Ask: “What’s on my Bring list?”
Stop using it

Paste your connector link to delete your sign-in from this connector. Then remove the connector on claude.ai.
